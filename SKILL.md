# Tanmay Bhat content playbook

Principles from Tanmay Bhat's interviews and talks, rewritten as working rules. The clips were
found via the fan page https://www.instagram.com/tanmaythinks_/, which reposts excerpts from
podcasts and panels. Each rule names its source clip; `INDEX.md` maps every clip id to its post.

## 1. Retention is the whole game

- Every format, long or short, lives or dies on watch time (average view duration). Write for
  "will they reach the last second", not "is this good". (`2026-09-25_DdtfzTTNy2I_1`)
- Build to a payoff at the end. Viral filters and trends all withhold the answer until the
  close; countdowns work because the best item comes last.
- Promise the payoff early, deliver it late. The opening line should make the viewer need the
  ending.
- Predictability rewards, surprise rewards more. The brain enjoys guessing what comes next; it
  enjoys a better-than-expected twist even more. Set up an expectation, then beat it.
  (`2026-09-28_Dd0_iCtBlAq_1`)

## 2. Design for the share button

- Before posting, ask which of the two shares this video earns (`2026-10-02_Dd_dODBzpB6_1`):
  - **Send to a friend**: "this is so you / so us". Built on a shared identity.
  - **Post to my profile**: the sharer uses it to signal who they are. Built on aspiration.
- Pick one per video and write toward it. A video built for neither gets watched, not shared.
- For professional audiences, "post to profile" usually wins: make the viewer look smart for
  passing it on.

## 3. Starting a page from zero

1. Pick a narrow niche and a memorable page name.
2. Do not post yet. Pull the top 10 videos from the biggest channels in that niche.
3. Cluster them: there are usually 5 or 6 recurring sub-topics. Those titles are proven demand.
4. Make your versions of those topics, optimising for watch time and shares (sections 1 and 2).

## 4. Find your "comedy plus X"

- Treat comedy (or any style) as the medium, not the content. Being interesting is the skill
  of holding attention; the subject is what you carry inside it. (`2026-09-23_DdooXuFSmLq_1`)
- If others are funnier or broader, compete on your combination: your style plus your real
  curiosity. Going deep on an unexpected subject is an edge. (`2026-10-04_DeE8mP3TQlu_1`)
- People are more multidimensional than their content. "Doctor and creator" beats choosing one.
  (`2026-09-26_DdwCFZwxnXv_1`)

## 5. Content is an asymmetric bet

- Most work pays back linearly. A post, like a book or a first date, can return far more than
  it cost. Volume of good attempts is the strategy. (`2026-09-26_DdwCFZwxnXv_1`)
- Beware variable rewards. One breakout makes you chase it in every video after; platforms
  benefit from that loop, you usually do not. Judge a video on its own job. (`2026-10-07_DeMYoJMTdBW_1`)

## 6. Fit before money

- Ask "do I enjoy making this" before "will this pay". Enjoyment fit comes first, business fit
  follows. Creating only because it is the thing to do burns out. (`2026-09-24_DdrW_s0zCFP_1`)
- Taking an audience from 0 to 10,000 proves you can build distribution and sell, and it beats a
  resume. Doing it 20 times builds the judgment most marketers lack.
  (`2026-09-30_Dd6g0w2zUVp_1`, `2026-10-01_Dd9Rxv5TAzC_1`)

## 7. Pitching and team

- Pitch by reading the work well and reading the room. Concede the line you can sense will not
  survive; giving ground builds the trust that gets the rest approved. (`2026-09-29_Dd3ynUGSRSJ_1`)
- Share upside with the team. When a sponsored piece pays, everyone on it gets a cut; good
  people stay. (`2026-10-02_Dd_3MbUTgWz_1`)

## Review checklist (run on every script)

- [ ] Does line one create a question only the ending answers?
- [ ] Is the best item or reveal last?
- [ ] Is there one twist that beats the obvious expectation?
- [ ] Which share is this built for: send-to-friend or post-to-profile?
- [ ] Is this a proven topic in the niche, or a guess?
- [ ] What is the "plus X": whose real curiosity is showing?

## Rules of use

- Borrow the mechanics, not the man. Do not imitate his voice, reuse his lines or jokes, or
  write as if he said something he did not.
- Never imply Tanmay Bhat endorses, sponsors or reviewed the content being made.
- When attributing an idea to him, link the original post from `INDEX.md`. If you have not
  checked the clip, say "according to a clip of Tanmay Bhat", not a direct quote.
