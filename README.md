## Who is Tanmay Bhat?

If you are outside India, you may not know the name. Inside India's internet, it is hard to
miss.

Tanmay Bhat is an Indian comedian, writer, YouTuber and producer. He co-founded **All India
Bakchod (AIB)**, the comedy collective that helped turn Indian YouTube into a mainstream stage
for sketch comedy and satire. He judged the first season of Amazon Prime's stand-up competition
**Comicstaan** (2018), became an early livestreamer, and today spends
much of his time advising creators, founders and brands on content and marketing.

What makes him worth studying is the mix. He has spent years in writers' rooms and on stage,
so he knows what holds a room. And he talks about content the way a growth team talks about a
funnel: watch time, shares, distribution, demand. This playbook is that second side of him,
pulled out of podcast and panel clips and turned into rules you can apply.

## What this skill does

Install it and Claude gets a working playbook for making content, built on his ideas:

| | Idea | In one line |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Retention is everything** | Write for the last second. Keep the screen alive. Save the reveal for the end. |
| 2 | **Design for the share** | Pick send-to-a-friend or post-to-my-profile, then pick the trigger that earns it. |
| 3 | **Start from proven demand** | Study the top 10 in your niche, start short, anchor on long-form. |
| 4 | **Find your angle** | Your style is the medium; your curiosity and point of view are the content. |
| 5 | **Reps beat talent** | Most drafts get binned. Loop count builds judgment, not years. |
| 6 | **Content is an asymmetric bet** | Small cost, uncapped upside. Keep a safe format next to every risky one. |
| 7 | **Fit before money** | Enjoy it first, keep the job until a year of runway, price on value. |
| 8 | **Team and pitching** | Generate 20, shortlist 5, cut hard, test on a room that looks like the audience. |
| 9 | **Content for brands** | Always on, one in-house owner of taste, judged on more than direct return. |
| 10 | **Outlast the algorithm** | Plan as if you had to stop posting in two years. |

Plus an eight-point checklist Claude runs on any script, hook or post you hand it.

## Install

```bash
git clone https://github.com/Harshil-Jani/tanmay-bhat-content.git ~/.claude/skills/tanmay-bhat-content
```

That is the whole install. Claude Code picks it up in every project.
